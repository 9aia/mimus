mimus is the org's verification engine — it enumerates the claims in your docs, comments, and kuda cards and proves each one or stays silent. It owns verification and nothing else: memory is kuda's, fixing is reconcile's (planned), distribution is tamias's.

  • Do a task → How-to — CI gate, kuda audit, rules authoring, multi-repo
  • Understand why → Architecture, claims & tiers, concepts, decisions
  • Look something up → CLI, config, violations, crate API
